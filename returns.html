Returns and refunds

Apex Hybrid Co. products are made to order and fulfilled by Fourthwall. This page matches Fourthwall's published return policy for its shops.

Change of mind or wrong size ordered

Fourthwall does not accept a return or refund because a customer changed their mind or ordered the wrong size. If you ordered the wrong size, the order is not eligible for a return.

Damage, a defective print, or the wrong size shipped

A replacement or refund is available when Fourthwall confirms a quality or fulfillment problem: a damaged item, a defective print, or a wrong size shipped by the production partner.

  • Report it within 30 days of the delivery date. Claims after that window are not processed, unless local law allows more time.
  • Email apexhybridco@gmail.com with the order number and photos of the item. Photos are required.
  • Fourthwall reviews the claim with the production partner.
  • If the claim is approved, the replacement ships at no charge. For a confirmed defect, the purchase price is refunded when a refund is the remedy, and return shipping for that defect is covered.

EU, EEA, and UK withdrawal

The made-to-order rule does not limit statutory withdrawal rights. An eligible customer in the EU, EEA, or UK may withdraw from an order of eligible physical goods within 14 days of receiving the last item, without giving a reason. Items made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalized are excluded.

The customer sends the withdrawal through the shop contact email or the contact path Fourthwall shows on the order. Goods go back within 14 days of the withdrawal, at the customer's cost, unless the item is defective. Fourthwall refunds the price and standard delivery within 14 days of the withdrawal and may wait until the goods are returned or proof they were sent. The refund can be reduced if the goods were handled more than needed to inspect them.
